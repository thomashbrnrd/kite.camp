# Known kite spots

`spots.json` is generated by `scripts/spots/build-spots.mjs` from `scripts/spots/seed.json`. Do not edit it by hand: change the seed and re-run `node scripts/spots/build-spots.mjs`.

## Sources and licences

- **Spot names**: [Wikipédia, « Spot de kitesurf »](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spot_de_kitesurf) and [Wikipedia NL, « Lijst van bekende kitesurfplekken »](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lijst_van_bekende_kitesurfplekken) (CC BY-SA 4.0), the project owner, and well-known spots added by hand (`nameSource` field).
- **Coordinates**: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) via Nominatim, or Wikidata (CC0) for the few manual entries (`coordinatesSource` field).

Because coordinates come from OpenStreetMap, `spots.json` is made available under the [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).

## Quality checks (2026-11-06)

- Every entry was geocoded with a country check and an expected-locality check; failures and suspicious matches were reviewed and fixed by hand (better query, search box, or Wikidata coordinates).
- Cross-check against GeoNames (Open-Meteo geocoding): 144/167 within 25 km of a GeoNames place with the same name or alias. The others were reviewed one by one: names unknown to GeoNames, overseas territories under another country code, or aliases pointing to a whole island.
- Coordinates point to the beach, lagoon or nearest village, not to the exact launch area. This is enough for the weather model, whose grid point is several kilometres away anyway (9–25 km resolution).
